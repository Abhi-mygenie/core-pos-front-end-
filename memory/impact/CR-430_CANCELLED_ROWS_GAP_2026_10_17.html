Why this was left behind — and what to fix

1
The spacer was added to make cancelled rows visually align with served rows (which had a checkmark icon at the end). The spacer is an empty div — completely invisible to the user. Its only job was to add space on the right to match the 44px checkmark column.
2
CR-430 fixed served rows by removing min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px] from the checkmark div. But cancelled rows still have this spacer — making every cancelled row 44px tall even though there is nothing inside the spacer.
3
The alignment reason is now gone — since served rows no longer have the large 44px icon, there is nothing to align to. The spacer in cancelled rows is now pointless AND still inflating the row height.
4
3-line fix: Remove min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px] from the spacer div (or replace the empty spacer with a tiny × icon for consistency with served rows' ✓). Change py-1.5 → py-0.5. Remove the "(Cancelled)" text label.
