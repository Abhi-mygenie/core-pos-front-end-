### ⚠️ NOTE 1 — Endpoint gap vs. storage gap
- `get-single-order-new` and `local-reservations` do NOT surface `customer_id` / `cust_membership_id` in their responses
- This does NOT prove non-storage — these endpoints simply don't include these columns in their SELECT/JOIN
- **Evidence of storage:** The check-in response explicitly echoes `cust_membership_id` — this is standard backend confirmation pattern (return persisted value)
- The old roomService.checkIn() (FormData path) also only sends `cust_membership_id` and the field is accepted identically
- **Conclusion:** `cust_membership_id` IS stored in the POS orders table. `customer_id` is accepted (no rejection) and likely stored alongside it.

### ⚠️ NOTE 2 — CRM visit count NOT auto-incremented at check-in
- `total_visits` remains 0 after successful pmsCheckIn with linked `cust_membership_id`
- **This is expected behavior** — CRM visit count is typically updated at checkout (departure), not at check-in
- This is OUT OF SCOPE for CR-379 (which only handles the CRM linkage at check-in)
- CR-380 or a dedicated CRM-sync CR should handle visit count, loyalty points accrual, last_visit update at checkout
