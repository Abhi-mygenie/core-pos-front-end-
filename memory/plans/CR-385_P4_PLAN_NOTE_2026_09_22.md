## 1. Plain-English plan
- **Bill** goes live on every In-House / Departures row and on the occupied Rooms tile. It opens **in place under the row** (like Extend): **LEFT** = the guest's statement, read from the server — Guest & stay → **ROOM** (collapsed heading shows `charge.total_with_gst`; open it: the per-night lines with their source chips (P3 `NightsLines`), booking amount, *Room upgrade* line when `charge.upgrade_amount > 0`, room-discount control **disabled** with tooltip "needs BQ-385-07", **SGST and CGST on two separate lines** (= BUG-418 fix), already paid, room balance) → **ROOM ORDERS** (room-service lines from the folio) → **TRANSFERRED** (dine-in bills posted to the room). **RIGHT** = the *existing* POS payment panel (`CollectPaymentPanel`, **not modified**), boxed at 440×560 so the **Checkout button is visible without scrolling on a 1366×768 laptop**; its three room-mode section toggles are hidden by host CSS only. Methods are the panel's own: Cash / Card / UPI / Split (by payment) / Credit = TAB.
- The panel's **room balance is fed from the LR row `charge.*`** (`balance_due`, `booking_charge`, `sgst+cgst`, `advance_payment`) — the BUG-425 hand formula in the old drawer is **not** copied — so the panel's room figure **equals the row's balance to the rupee** (AC-02 / M6-10). Paying → `POST order-bill-payment` through the copied drawer handler → toast **"Checked out · Room rX"** → row leaves the table, tile turns Needs cleaning. A second payment on a paid order answers 200 `status:"already_paid"` → toast "Already checked out", no error, no second ledger row (M6-04). Print Bill = panel's own; Print Folio disabled (OD-385-15). No Undo (D1).
- Nothing else changes: legacy `/pms/departures` drawer, POS `/dashboard` checkout, `pmsService`, transforms — all byte-identical.
