## AREA 1 — MENU TYPES & SWITCHING (FAQ 1–5)

---

### FAQ-01: How do I switch between Normal and Party menu?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Menu Management landing, Normal selected]**
Your restaurant can run multiple menus at the same time — Normal, Party, Premium and Aggregator. To switch, look at the dropdown at the top of the Menu Management page. It currently shows "Normal."

**[Action — click the dropdown]**
Click the dropdown and select "Party." The page reloads and now shows only the categories and items belonging to your Party menu — with the Party prices.

**[Closing]**
Everything you see — item counts, prices, availability — now belongs to the Party menu. Switch back to Normal the same way, anytime.

---

### FAQ-02: Can I have different prices on the Party or Premium menu?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Normal menu visible]**
Yes — Party and Premium are completely independent price lists. Switch to the Party menu using the dropdown at the top.

**[Action — switch to Party]**
Select "Party." Now find the item you want to price differently — for example, Butter Chicken. Click the lightning bolt icon to open Quick Edit.

**[Action — change price]**
Change the price to your Party rate. Click Save. This price only applies when the POS is on Party menu — your Normal menu price is untouched.

**[Closing]**
Repeat for every item where your Party or Premium price differs. Each menu holds its own independent set of prices.

---

### FAQ-03: What is the Aggregator menu — how is it different from Normal?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — dropdown visible]**
The Aggregator menu is a separate catalogue used specifically for Swiggy and Zomato. It is not shown on your in-house POS order screen — it is only pushed to the delivery platforms.

**[Action — switch to Aggregator]**
Select "Aggregator" from the dropdown. You will see your items with platform-specific stock controls and sync options.

**[Closing]**
Use the Aggregator menu to control what appears on delivery platforms, set platform-specific images, and mark items out of stock on Swiggy or Zomato without affecting your dine-in menu. Changes here do not change your Normal menu.

---

### FAQ-04: How does a POS station know which menu to use?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Menu Management page, Local Settings section visible]**
Each POS station or tablet can be set to use a specific menu independently. This is done through Local Settings inside Menu Management.

**[Action — scroll to bottom, click Active Menu Selector]**
At the bottom of the menu panel, find the Active Menu section. Click the dropdown and select which menu this station should use — Normal, Party, Premium or Aggregator.

**[Closing]**
Once saved, every order taken on this station will use that menu's prices and availability. Your other stations are unaffected. This is useful when one counter runs a bar menu while another runs the food menu.

---

### FAQ-05: How do I set which menu a specific station or terminal uses?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — inside Menu Management]**
Go to Menu Management. Scroll to the Local Settings section at the bottom of the panel.

**[Action — Active Menu dropdown]**
Click the Active Menu dropdown and pick the menu — for example, Premium. This setting is saved per device, so this terminal will now default to the Premium menu every time it opens Menu Management or the order screen.

**[Closing]**
Each terminal remembers its own setting. Change it anytime from the same Local Settings section.

## AREA 2 — CATEGORIES (FAQ 6–10)

---

### FAQ-06: How do I add a new category?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — category column visible]**
In Menu Management, look at the left column — this is your category list. Scroll to the bottom of it.

**[Action — click + Add Category]**
Click the green "+ Add Category" button. An inline form appears. Type the name of your new category — for example, "Seasonal Specials."

**[Action — choose station, click Add]**
Select the kitchen station or printer this category's orders should go to. Click Add. Your new category appears instantly with a zero item count.

**[Closing]**
You can now start adding items to this category.

---

### FAQ-07: How do I rename a category?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — category list visible]**
Hover over the category you want to rename. Two icons appear on the right — a pencil and a trash bin.

**[Action — click pencil]**
Click the pencil. The category row turns into an inline edit form. Clear the current name and type the new one.

**[Action — press Enter or click Save]**
Press Enter or click Save. The name updates immediately across the entire system — the POS order screen will reflect this change right away.

---

### FAQ-08: How do I delete a category?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — category list]**
Hover over the category you want to remove. Click the red trash icon on the right.

**[Action — confirmation strip appears]**
A small "Delete?" confirmation appears right in the row. Click "Yes" to confirm.

**[Important note]**
Note: if the category still has items in it, move those items to another category first — a category with items cannot be deleted until it is empty.

**[Closing]**
Once confirmed, the category is removed from the menu and the POS.

---

### FAQ-09: How do I change which kitchen printer a category routes to?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — category list, station label visible under category name]**
Each category has a small grey label showing its kitchen station. This controls where KOT tickets print for items in that category.

**[Action — hover, click pencil]**
Hover the category, click the pencil to open the inline edit. Next to the name field is the Station dropdown.

**[Action — change station]**
Click the dropdown and pick the correct station — for example, change from "KDS" to "Bar Printer." The printer hint below shows you which physical printer that station maps to.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save. From this point, every KOT for this category prints to the Bar Printer.

---

### FAQ-10: How do I change the order categories appear on the POS?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — category list with drag grips visible]**
Categories appear on the POS order screen in exactly the order you set here. To reorder them, use the drag grip — the double-dot icon on the left of each category row.

**[Action — drag category up or down]**
Click and hold the grip on the category you want to move. Drag it up or down. A gap shows where it will land. Release to drop it.

**[Closing]**
The new order saves automatically. Open the POS order screen and your categories will appear in this order.

## AREA 3 — ITEM BROWSE, SEARCH & FILTER (FAQ 11–13)

---

### FAQ-11: How do I search for a specific item?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Menu Management, item column visible]**
In the right column of Menu Management, you will see a search box above the item cards. Click it and start typing the item name.

**[Action — type item name]**
As you type, the list filters in real time. You do not need to press Enter. The matching items appear instantly.

**[Closing]**
Click any item to open its Quick Edit or Full Edit. Clear the search box to return to the full list.

---

### FAQ-12: How do I see only inactive or switched-off items?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — item column]**
Click the orange "Filters" button above the item cards. A row of filter chips appears.

**[Action — click Inactive]**
Under Status, click "Inactive." The list now shows only items that have been switched off. Their cards appear greyed out with an "Inactive" tag.

**[Closing]**
From here you can reactivate any item by clicking the power icon on its card, or open it for editing. Click "All" under Status to clear the filter.

---

### FAQ-13: How do I filter items by Veg or Non-Veg?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — item column]**
Click the "Filters" button. Under Food Type, you will see chips: All, Veg, Non-Veg, Egg, Jain.

**[Action — click Non-Veg]**
Click "Non-Veg." The list now shows only non-vegetarian items. The chip turns orange to show the filter is active.

**[Closing]**
You can combine filters — for example, Inactive + Non-Veg at the same time. Click "All" under each group to clear. Click the Filters button again to hide the chip rows.

## AREA 4 — ADDING A NEW ITEM (FAQ 14–16)

---

### FAQ-14: How do I add a new food item to the menu?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Menu Management, item column]**
To add a new item, click the green "+ Add" button at the top of the item column. The Full Edit form opens, completely empty.

**[Action — fill required fields]**
Three fields are required: Name, Category and Price. Fill in the item name, select a category from the dropdown, and enter the base price.

**[Action — set food type]**
Set the Food Type — Veg, Non-Veg, Egg or Jain. This determines the colour-coded dot that appears on every order ticket and card.

**[Action — click Save Item]**
Click "Save Item" at the bottom. The item is created and appears in the card view immediately.

**[Closing]**
Everything else — images, variations, add-ons, availability — can be added later by opening the item in Full Edit.

---

### FAQ-15: What information is required when adding an item?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — blank Add Item form]**
When adding a new item, only three fields are mandatory: the item Name, its Category, and the base Price. Everything else has a sensible default.

**[Defaults callout]**
By default, the item is set to Veg, GST tax type, all channels enabled (Dine-In, Delivery, Takeaway), and no variations or add-ons attached.

**[Closing]**
Fill the three required fields and click Save. You can always come back to fill in images, descriptions, variations and add-ons later.

---

### FAQ-16: How do I add an item to a specific menu type — for example, Party or Premium?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Menu Management]**
Before adding the item, switch to the menu you want it in. Use the dropdown at the top — select "Party."

**[Action — click + Add]**
Now click "+ Add." The form that opens will create the item inside the Party menu. Set the name, category, and the Party-specific price.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save. The item now exists only in the Party menu. Switch back to Normal and it will not appear there.

**[Closing]**
To have the same item on both Normal and Party menus with different prices, add it separately to each menu. Each menu maintains its own independent item list.

### FAQ-20: How do I mark an item as Complementary — given free to guests?

## AREA 7 — FULL EDIT: PRICING, TAX & DISCOUNTS (FAQ 24–27)

---

### FAQ-24: How do I set GST or VAT on an item?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Full Edit, Pricing & Tax section]**
In the Full Edit form, find the Pricing & Tax section. Click it to expand if it is collapsed.

**[Action — Tax Type dropdown]**
Click the Tax Type dropdown and select GST or VAT. A Tax % field appears next to it.

**[Action — enter rate]**
Enter the applicable rate — for example, 5% for food, 18% for beverages. Then choose Tax Calc: "Exclusive" if you want tax added on top of the price displayed, or "Inclusive" if the price already includes tax.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item. Tax is now applied to every order of this item.

---

### FAQ-25: What is the difference between Inclusive and Exclusive tax?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Tax Calc field in Pricing & Tax]**
When setting tax on an item, you must choose between Exclusive and Inclusive.

**[Exclusive explanation]**
Exclusive means the price you enter is the base price before tax. If you enter ₹100 with 5% GST Exclusive, the guest pays ₹105 — ₹100 item + ₹5 tax shown separately.

**[Inclusive explanation]**
Inclusive means the price already includes the tax. If you enter ₹105 with 5% GST Inclusive, the guest pays ₹105 total — and ₹5 of that is the GST component, calculated backwards.

**[Closing]**
Most restaurants use Exclusive. Use Inclusive when you want to advertise a round price like ₹199 that already covers tax.

---

### FAQ-26: How do I allow a discount to be applied to a specific item?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Pricing & Tax section]**
In Full Edit, Pricing & Tax section, find the "Allow Discount" toggle. By default it is off.

**[Action — toggle on]**
Turn it on. Two new fields appear: Discount amount and Discount Type.

**[Action — set discount]**
Set the type — Flat (a fixed rupee amount) or Percent (a percentage off). Enter the value.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item. This item can now receive the discount when it is added to an order.

---

### FAQ-27: How do I set a flat versus percentage discount on an item?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Allow Discount toggled on, fields visible]**
With Allow Discount switched on in the Full Edit form, you will see the Discount Type dropdown.

**[Action — select Flat]**
Select "Flat" to give a fixed rupee discount — for example, ₹20 off. Or select "Percent" to give a percentage — for example, 10% off the item price.

**[Action — enter value and Save]**
Enter the discount value and click Save Item. The correct discount type will apply when a cashier uses this item in an order.

## AREA 8 — FULL EDIT: AVAILABILITY & CHANNELS (FAQ 28–31)

---

**[Action — turn off Dine-In and Takeaway]**
Switch Dine-In and Takeaway OFF. Leave Delivery ON.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item. This item will only appear on Delivery orders. Cashiers on Dine-In tables will not see it, and it will be hidden from Takeaway orders.

---

### FAQ-29: How do I publish an item to the online ordering page?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Availability & Channels section]**
Open the item in Full Edit. In the Availability & Channels section, find the "Live Web (Online Ordering)" toggle.

**[Action — turn on]**
Switch it ON. Click Save Item.

**[Closing]**
The item will now appear on your restaurant's online ordering page for guests to order directly. Make sure the item also has a description and photo for the best online experience.

---

### FAQ-30: How do I restrict an item to breakfast hours only?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Availability & Channels section]**
Open the item in Full Edit and expand Availability & Channels. Below the channel toggles, find the "Available From" and "Available Until" time fields.

**[Action — set time window]**
Click "Available From" and set 07:00. Click "Available Until" and set 11:00.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item.

**[Closing]**
Outside of 7 AM to 11 AM, this item is automatically hidden from the POS order screen. No manual switching needed — the system handles it by the clock.

---

### FAQ-31: How do I remove an item from Takeaway orders?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Availability & Channels section]**
Open the item in Full Edit, expand Availability & Channels. Find the Takeaway toggle — it is ON by default.

**[Action — switch off]**
Switch Takeaway OFF. Click Save Item.

**[Closing]**
The item will no longer appear when a cashier starts a Takeaway or Parcel order. It remains available for Dine-In and Delivery unless those are also switched off.

## AREA 9 — FULL EDIT: VARIATIONS (FAQ 32–35)

---

### FAQ-32: How do I add size options — Small, Medium, Large — to an item?
**Duration:** ~60s

**[Opening — Full Edit, Food Variations section]**
Open the item in Full Edit and expand the Food Variations section. Click "+ Add New Variation."

**[Action — name the group]**
Give the variation group a name: "Size." Set the type to "Single" — the guest picks exactly one option.

**[Action — add options]**
Click "+ Add option." Type "Small" and set its extra price to 0. Add another: "Medium" at ₹30 extra. Add one more: "Large" at ₹60 extra.

**[Action — Required toggle]**
If you want the cashier to always choose a size before adding to the order, toggle "Required" ON.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item. The Size option will now appear every time this item is added to an order.

---

### FAQ-33: How do I add a spice level option and make it mandatory?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Food Variations section]**
In Full Edit, expand Food Variations and click "+ Add New Variation." Name it "Spice Level." Set type to "Single."

**[Action — add options]**
Add three options: "Mild" at ₹0, "Medium" at ₹0, and "Hot" at ₹0. No extra charge — it is just a preference.

**[Action — Required ON]**
Toggle "Required" ON. The cashier must select a spice level before the item can be added to the order.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item. This ensures spice preference is always captured for the kitchen.

---

### FAQ-34: How do I charge extra for a specific variation option?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Food Variations section with existing variation]**
Open the item in Full Edit. In the Food Variations section, find the variation group. Each option row has a name and a price field.

**[Action — set extra price]**
Click the price field next to the option you want to charge for — for example, "Large" — and enter the extra amount, say ₹50.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item. When a guest selects Large, ₹50 is automatically added to the item price on the order.

---

### FAQ-35: How do I allow a guest to pick multiple variation options?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Food Variations, editing a variation group]**
In the Food Variations section, find the Single / Multi selector for the variation group. Change it from "Single" to "Multi."

**[Action — set Min and Max]**
Two new fields appear: Min and Max. Set Min to 1 (must pick at least one) and Max to 3 (can pick up to three). Adjust these to your preference.

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item. The cashier can now select multiple options from this group — for example, multiple toppings on a pizza.

---

**[Action — toggle ON]**
Switch it ON. Click Save Item.

**[Closing]**
The item disappears from the cashier's order screen but remains in your menu database. It can still appear on online ordering if you choose. Toggle it back OFF to make it visible on the POS again — useful for seasonal or test items.

---

Open the item in Full Edit and expand the Operations section. Find the "Prep Time (min)" field.

**[Action — enter prep time]**

**[Action — Save]**
Click Save Item.

**[Closing]**

---

---

### FAQ-43: How do I link an item to inventory so stock is auto-deducted?

**[Action — toggle ON]**

## AREA 12 — DELETING AN ITEM (FAQ 44–46)

---

### FAQ-44: How do I delete a food item?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — item card]**
Hover over the item you want to delete. Four icons appear on the right. Click the red trash icon — the last one.

**[Action — confirmation strip]**
A confirmation strip opens inside the card: "Delete [item name]?" with a mandatory reason dropdown and Cancel / Delete buttons.

**[Action — select reason and Delete]**
Select a reason from the dropdown — for example, "Discontinued." The Delete button activates. Click Delete.

**[Closing]**
The item is permanently removed from the menu.

---

### FAQ-45: Why does it ask for a reason when I delete an item?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — delete confirmation visible]**
When you click the trash icon, you are required to choose a reason before the Delete button becomes active. This is intentional.

**[Explanation]**
MyGenie records why items are removed for audit purposes — so owners and managers can track whether items were discontinued, replaced, entered in error, or removed for another reason.

**[Closing]**
Select the reason that applies, then click Delete. The reason is stored in the item's audit trail and can be reviewed in the audit reports.

---

### FAQ-46: Can I recover or undo a deleted item?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — empty item list after deletion]**
Once an item is deleted, it cannot be recovered from Menu Management directly. There is no undo button.

**[Alternative — deactivate instead]**
If you think you might need the item again, consider deactivating it instead of deleting. Hover the item, click the power icon — it becomes Inactive and disappears from the POS order screen, but stays in your database.

**[Closing]**
Reserve deletion for items you are certain you will never need again. Use Inactive for items you want to hide temporarily.

## AREA 14 — BULK EDITOR: GRID & COLUMNS (FAQ 51–54)

---

### FAQ-51: How do I edit prices for many items at once?
**Duration:** ~60s

**[Opening — Menu Management header]**
Click the "Bulk Edit" button in the Menu Management header. The view switches to a spreadsheet-style grid showing all your items as rows.

**[Action — find price column]**
Each row is an item. Find the Price column. Click any price cell to edit it directly — type the new value and press Tab to move to the next.

**[Action — dirty cells turn orange]**
As you edit, changed cells turn orange. This shows you what has been modified but not yet saved.

**[Action — click Save]**
When you are done, click Save at the bottom. All changes are committed in one go.

**[Closing]**
Bulk Edit is the fastest way to do a price revision — you can update dozens of items in minutes without opening individual forms.

---

### FAQ-52: How do I choose which columns appear in the bulk editor?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Bulk Editor grid]**
In the Bulk Editor, click the "Columns" button at the top. A column picker opens showing available columns grouped by tier: Basic, Pricing, Tax, Classification, and more.

**[Action — tick/untick columns]**
Tick the columns you need for this session — for example, Price, Tax Type and Tax %. Untick the ones you do not need, like Variations or Add-ons, to keep the grid clean.

**[Action — close picker]**
Press Escape or click outside. The grid updates to show only your chosen columns.

---

### FAQ-53: What does "Validate Tax" do in the bulk editor?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Bulk Editor grid]**
At the top of the Bulk Editor, next to the Columns button, is "Validate Tax." Click it.

**[What it does]**
It checks every visible item's tax configuration against your restaurant's registered tax slabs. If an item has a tax rate that does not match any valid slab — for example, 7% when only 5% and 18% are valid — it is highlighted.

**[Closing]**
Use this after importing from Excel or after a bulk price revision to catch any tax misconfiguration before it affects a guest bill. Correct highlighted items and validate again.

---

### FAQ-54: How do I save all my bulk changes at once?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Bulk Editor with orange dirty cells]**
As you edit cells in the Bulk Editor, they turn orange — these are your unsaved changes. You can see a count of dirty cells at the top.

**[Action — click Save]**
When you are satisfied, click the Save button at the bottom of the grid. All orange cells are committed to the menu in one API call.

**[Reset option]**
If you want to discard all changes and start over, click "Reset All." All orange cells return to their original values.

### FAQ-59: Why does bulk delete ask for a reason?

**[Explanation]**
This keeps your menu audit trail clean. If 20 items are removed in one go, the system records why — discontinued, seasonal end, pricing error — so management can review the decision later.

**[Closing]**
Select the most appropriate reason from the dropdown and click Confirm. The reason is logged against all deleted items.

**[Action — tick header checkbox]**

**[Action — Delete Selected]**
Click "Delete Selected," choose a reason, and confirm. All items from that category are deleted.

---

### FAQ-61: How do I mark an item as out of stock on Swiggy or Zomato?
**Duration:** ~45s

**[Opening — Aggregator menu selected]**
Switch to the Aggregator menu using the dropdown. Find the item you want to mark as unavailable.

**[Action — click stock toggle on card]**
On the item card, you will see platform availability chips — Swiggy and Zomato. Click the stock toggle for the platform you want to disable — for example, click the Swiggy toggle.

**[Action — popover appears]**
A popover opens asking how long to disable it: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, or Indefinitely.

**[Action — select and confirm]**
Select your duration and click Disable. The item is immediately marked as unavailable on Swiggy for that period.

---

### FAQ-62: How do I set an item to automatically come back in stock after 30 minutes?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — stock disable popover]**
When you click the stock toggle for Swiggy or Zomato on an item, a popover appears with time options.

**[Action — select 30 minutes]**
Select "30 minutes" and click Disable.

**[Closing]**
The item is marked out of stock on the platform. After 30 minutes, the system automatically re-enables it without any manual action needed. You can also select 1 hour or 4 hours for longer windows.

---

### FAQ-63: How do I disable an item on Zomato without affecting the normal menu?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — Aggregator menu, item card]**
Switch to the Aggregator menu. Find the item. The Aggregator menu is entirely separate from your Normal menu — changes here never touch the Normal, Party, or Premium menus.

**[Action — click Zomato stock toggle]**
Click the Zomato stock chip on the item card and select a disable duration.

**[Closing]**
The item disappears from Zomato immediately. Your Normal menu is completely unaffected — dine-in and takeaway orders can still include this item. Re-enable anytime by clicking the toggle again.

## AREA 20 — ITEM REORDERING (FAQ 69–70)

---

### FAQ-69: How do I change the order items appear on the POS order screen?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — item cards for a category]**
Select a category from the left column. The items for that category appear as cards on the right. Each card has a drag grip — the double-dot icon — on its left edge.

**[Action — drag item]**
Click and hold the grip on the item you want to move. Drag it up or down. The card turns orange while lifted and a gap shows the landing position.

**[Action — release]**
Release to drop. The new order saves automatically. Open the POS order screen and items appear in this exact sequence.

---

### FAQ-70: Does the drag-and-drop item order affect the guest-facing online menu?
**Duration:** ~30s

**[Opening — reordered items visible]**
Yes. The order you set in Menu Management is reflected everywhere this menu is displayed — both on the in-house POS order screen and on the Live Web online ordering page.

**[Callout — aggregator platforms]**
For Swiggy and Zomato, the order is controlled by their own platforms after the menu is synced — the aggregator platforms may reorder items according to their own display rules.

**[Closing]**
For the best guest experience on your own ordering page, put your most popular and high-margin items at the top of each category.
