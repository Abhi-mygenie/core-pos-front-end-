Design Idea: 3 Levels of Detail

Owner opens page → sees what's wrong → taps to understand → scrolls for evidence

🚨
Level 1 — What's Wrong
3 big alert cards. Red = act now. Amber = watch. Owner reads headline numbers in 3 seconds.
📈
Level 2 — How Bad Is It
Risk distribution bar, fraud pattern breakdown, discount manipulation summary. Visual, not numbers.
🔍
Level 3 — Show Me Everything
Expandable drill-down tables per category. Full event detail, timestamps, amounts. Only when owner wants it.
vs Current Design (v1 mockup):
  • v1: 15 KPI tiles dumped in 4 sections → owner has to scan and do math to understand severity
  • v2: 3 alert cards with plain English → owner sees "44 High Risk Cancellations" and knows immediately
  • v1: One giant table with all 121 events → overwhelming
  • v2: Drill-down per category, collapsed by default → clean, focused

Why this works:

  • Plain English descriptions — no jargon, no codes, no "late_cancel_high_risk_count"
  • Red = act now (cancellations, fraud). Purple = investigate (discounts). Color = urgency.
  • Existing CR-528 counters (Make Unpaid, Pay Change, Post-Print Edits) stay as a quiet stats bar — they're context, not the headline
  • Each card is clickable → scrolls to drill-down

Key design choices:
  • Risk bar — one horizontal bar shows distribution visually. Owner sees "almost half is red" without reading any number.
  • Insight sentence — "43% of cancellations are High Risk" tells the owner what to do (investigate).
  • Fraud cards — CR-544 and CR-546 get summary cards with plain English. "1 order, unpaid 3 times, cancelled 2 days later" tells a clear story.
  • No raw KPI tiles — everything is contextual and interpreted.

Key drill-down design choices:
  • High Risk first — drill-downs sorted by severity. Owner sees worst cases at top.
  • Collapsed by default — OK cancellations (49 items) are hidden unless owner explicitly wants them. Keeps page clean.
  • Human-readable time gaps — "2 days 15 min" instead of "2,895 min". Owner shouldn't do math.
  • CR-546 "Replaced" rows merged — instead of showing 2 separate rows (remove old + apply new), show 1 row: "10% (₹2) → Fixed ₹3" with purple highlight.
  • Category-specific columns — each drill-down shows only its relevant columns (no "—" spam).

Design Decisions for Approval

OD-DESIGN-01: Should "OK Cancellations" (49 items, 0–4 min) be shown at all, or hidden behind a "Show safe cancellations" toggle? They're not suspicious — showing them might dilute the signal.
OD-DESIGN-02: For CR-546 "Replace" events — the backend sends 2 separate rows (remove old + apply new). Should the frontend merge them into 1 row showing "Old → New" (cleaner), or keep as 2 rows (matches raw data)?
OD-DESIGN-03: Should there be a "Download Report" that generates a PDF/Excel with just the suspicious events (High Risk + Cancel After Unpaid + Discount Changes), excluding OK cancellations? Useful for sharing with management.
Summary of the v2 design approach:
  • Level 1: 3 alert cards (plain English, big numbers, color-coded urgency)
  • Level 2: Risk bar + fraud summary cards (visual, interpreted, no raw KPI tiles)
  • Level 3: Drill-down tables per category (collapsed by default, severity-sorted, human-readable gaps)
  • Existing CR-528 events: Quiet stats bar at top (not removed, just deprioritized)
