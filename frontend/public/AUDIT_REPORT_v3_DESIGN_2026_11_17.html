Approach C Overlap Tags — how they work:
  • "Also In" column shows colored pills for every other card this event qualifies for
  • ⏲ High Risk = also in Card 1 (cancel duration ≥ 5 min)
  • 🔒 After Unpaid = also in Card 2 (order was marked unpaid)
  • 🖨 After Print = also in Card 3 (bill was already printed)
  • Events with 2 or 3 tags = highest priority for investigation
  • Each card's ₹ leakage counts ALL its events (including overlapping ones)
  • Top-level Total Events (121) is deduplicated — each event counted once
