App updated — reloading shortly Or tap Reload now to update immediately
Behaviour
Position: Fixed to the bottom-centre of the screen — never blocks the order table or collect bill panel.

Countdown: Green ring ticks from 10 → 0. At 0, page reloads automatically.

Reload now button: Tapping reloads immediately without waiting.

During reload: The page briefly goes blank (≈ 1–2 s) then the fresh app loads. The user lands on the same route they were on.

Cashier mid-order: If a cashier has a bill open, the order is already saved server-side. After reload they reopen the order — nothing is lost.

Z-index: Banner is always on top — even above modals and overlays.
